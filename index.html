Get started · about a minute

Three steps, done once

1

Add the bookmark

NeggSweeper Helper

Drag the orange button up to your bookmarks bar (favorites bar in Edge). No bar? Press Ctrl+Shift+B.

NeggSweeper Helper
2

Open the game

neopets.com/games/neggsweeper

Go to NeggSweeper on Neopets and start a board, any level.

Open the game ↗
3

Click the bookmark

Once, on the game page. The negg to click next glows, with a label pointing at it. It updates after every click, win and new game until you close the tab.

Green: safeClick any of them.
Red: bad neggLeave it alone.
Orange: best betNothing is certain; this is the least likely to be bad.

The card in the corner of the game counts the safe neggs and can show the odds on every negg. Its × puts the page back to normal. Works in Chrome, Edge and Firefox on Windows and Mac.
